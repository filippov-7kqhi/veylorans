About Veylorans

Language Learning That Actually Talks Back

Founded by polyglot language coach Elif Duran, Veylorans was built on a simple belief: you learn to speak by speaking, with a real person correcting and encouraging you in real time — not by memorizing another app's flashcards alone.

  • Native and near-native certified language coaches
  • Coaching in 14 languages, from beginner to fluent
  • Exam prep for IELTS, TOEFL, DELF and more
  • Every session built around real conversation practice
